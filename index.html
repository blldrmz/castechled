CUSTOM LED SCREENS

Casino LED screens
and LED video walls.

CasTechLED by Virexa builds fully customized indoor and outdoor LED screens for casinos, hotels, retail and hospitality venues. We offer a range of pixel pitches, from fine-pitch high resolution screens for close viewing to larger pitches for long-distance outdoor displays.

Viewing distance decides the right pixel pitch, so we help you get sharp images without paying for resolution you don't need. Size, shape, brightness and mounting are all made to your project.
