Policy.

How we handle personal data sent through this website and by email.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who is responsible

The controller of personal data collected through castechled.com is Virexa AI Technologies s.r.o., operating the CasTechLED brand from Prague, Czech Republic. Contact: info@castechled.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Quote and contact requests: name, email, phone, company, project location and the project details you enter. We use them to answer your request and prepare a quotation (legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Email and phone communication: the content you send us, kept to handle your enquiry and the resulting business relationship.

3. Cookies and third-party content

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is transmitted to Google when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub also processes technical request data (such as IP address) to deliver pages.

4. Who receives your data

Our email provider, the service that processes website form submissions (once enabled), and, where needed for your project, suppliers and installation partners. We do not sell personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a contract are deleted after 24 months at the latest. Data tied to contracts and invoices is kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to info@castechled.com. You may also complain to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (uoou.gov.cz).
