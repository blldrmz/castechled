CASE STUDIES

From the first sketch
to the first broadcast.

Each project below is documented with our own photographs and site videos, from structure and lifting to calibration and live content.

Pixel pitch, brightness and protection class are selected for each location and confirmed in the individual quotation.

01 · OUTDOOR · CURVED LED · BATUMI, GEORGIA

Curved corner façade display

A rounded building corner on a busy street, turned into a continuous wrap-around screen.

The brief

The client wanted an advertising surface on the rounded corner of a residential building, without changing the shape of the façade and visible from both streets.

What we did

We fixed a steel sub-frame to the façade, lifted the curved outdoor cabinets into place with a crane and set them to follow the corner radius. Every cabinet was checked with a full-white calibration test before handover.

The result

One seamless, curved image that reads from both directions and keeps its colour in direct sunlight.

02 · OUTDOOR · BRIDGE-MOUNTED · BATUMI, GEORGIA

Pedestrian bridge billboard

A wide-format LED billboard installed on a pedestrian bridge above a live road.

The brief

Install a large billboard on an existing pedestrian bridge over a main road, with as little disruption to traffic as possible.

What we did

Cabinets arrived in flight cases and were lifted into position. Mounting was done at night from a boom lift behind a coned-off lane, with power and data routed along the bridge structure.

The result

A bright, wide screen facing approaching traffic, readable in daylight and at night.

03 · OUTDOOR · CURVED · HIGH-RISE · BATUMI, GEORGIA

Rooftop curved LED landmark

A large curved display on a high-rise terrace, visible across the skyline.

The brief

Create a landmark screen on the terrace of a high-rise building, curved to be seen from a wide angle across the city.

What we did

We assembled a rigid steel structure on the terrace, lifted the cabinet columns by crane and enclosed the rear with weatherproof cladding.

The result

A curved screen that becomes a night-time landmark among the surrounding towers.

04 · OUTDOOR · FREE-STANDING BILLBOARD · BATUMI, GEORGIA

Free-standing roadside billboard

Built and tested in our workshop, set on site by crane in a single night.

The brief

A large free-standing billboard on a main road, installed quickly so the site was blocked for as short a time as possible.

What we did

The screen and its frame were assembled and tested in the workshop, transported in one piece and set onto the column by crane at night. Only final connection and content setup remained on site.

The result

A high-brightness billboard visible from the road, installed with minimal time on site.

05 · OUTDOOR · CURVED FAÇADE BAND · BATUMI, GEORGIA

Shopping mall curved façade band

An LED band that follows the curved glass façade above a shopping mall entrance.

The brief

Add a brand advertising band along the curved façade above the main entrance, matched to the existing architecture.

What we did

Cabinets were installed along the façade curve from a boom lift while the building stayed open, with project signage on site during the works.

A continuous advertising band across the full curve of the building.

WORKSHOP TO SITE

Tested before it ships.
Protected until it is installed.

The part of a project you do not see on the street: how each display is prepared before installation day.

01

Build and test

Every screen is assembled and run before it leaves us: modules, receiving cards and power are checked on the full configuration.

02

Pack and protect

Cabinets travel in our own branded flight cases, so they arrive in the same condition they left the test rack.

03

Deliver to site

Cases are delivered to site, lifted where needed and opened only at the point of installation.

04

Custom fabrication

Frames, stands and enclosures are made in the workshop to fit each location, from slim poster stands to box enclosures.
